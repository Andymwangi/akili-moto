# Word lists

Owner: Anderson Mwangi
Status: machine-built candidates, awaiting human review. Nothing here is final.

## What these files are

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `dictionary-candidates.txt` | Permissive list of 5-letter words accepted as valid guesses. Built to avoid rejecting real words, so it is deliberately generous and contains some noise. |
| `answers-shortlist.csv` | Ranked candidates for the daily answer. A reviewer marks `keep` as `y` for words that are fair, common, and clean. |
| `blocklist.txt` | Words that must never appear as answers or guesses. Empty, to be filled by a fluent reviewer. |
| `dictionary-remove.txt` | Optional list of junk or inappropriate words to drop from the dictionary. |
| `build_wordlists.py` | Reproducible build script. Same inputs give the same outputs. |
| `finalize_answers.py` | Turns the reviewed CSV into final `answers.txt` and `dictionary.txt` and validates them. |
| `fetch_sources.sh` | Downloads the open source files used as input. |
| `build-stats.txt` | Counts and thresholds from the last build. |

## How the candidate lists were built

1. Keep only words that are lowercase a to z and exactly 5 letters (decision D5). Words with apostrophes such as ng' are not rewritten or merged.
2. **Dictionary** = Hunspell lowercase entries, plus ASP words, plus corpus words seen at least 2 times with no strong proper-noun signal, plus a few prefix-expanded Hunspell forms, plus capitalized-only Hunspell entries only when they are common in lowercase use.
3. **Answer candidates** = dictionary words with a combined frequency score of at least 3, backed by Hunspell or the storybook corpus (not corpus noise alone), with a low rate of mid-sentence capitalization (a proper-noun signal).
4. Rank by score (Common Voice frequency plus twice the ASP frequency).

Thresholds are at the top of `build_wordlists.py`.

## Known weaknesses (why review is mandatory)

- **Proper nouns:** the capitalization check catches many names but not all.
- **Grammatical forms:** Swahili inflects heavily. The list contains verb forms, possessives, and function words (for example forms like `yangu` or `hivyo`) that are valid words but poor daily answers.
- **Noise:** the Hunspell list is a spellchecker list built partly from web crawling, so it contains some typos and non-Swahili words. This mostly affects the dictionary, where being generous is safer than rejecting real words.
- **Dialect and register:** standard Swahili only. Sheng is out of scope for v1.
- **Offensive words:** not filtered. That needs a human.

## Reviewer instructions

For the answer shortlist, open `answers-shortlist.csv` in Google Sheets or Excel and set `keep` to `y` only for words that are:

1. Common enough that an adult fluent speaker would recognize them without hesitation
2. A real word on its own, not just a fragment of a verb, and not a pure grammatical particle
3. Not a person's name, place name, brand, or abbreviation
4. Not offensive, vulgar, or hurtful
5. Not an English word or loanword you would not want as a Swahili puzzle answer

Use `notes` for doubts. Aim for at least 365 approved answers, and ideally 450 or more as a buffer.

Also:
- Add any offensive words you see anywhere in either list to `blocklist.txt`.
- Add junk words you notice in `dictionary-candidates.txt` to `dictionary-remove.txt`. Skim the dictionary for the obvious ones, but do not try to review every word.

Then run:

```bash
python3 finalize_answers.py --dir out
```

It writes `answers.txt` and `dictionary.txt` and warns if fewer than 365 answers are approved. Copy those two files into the game repository's `data/` folder.
