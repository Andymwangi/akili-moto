# Quiz question bank

## Status

`data/quiz-questions.json` is a sourced starter draft for local MVP play. The owner and a fluent Swahili reviewer should review question wording, translations, choices, explanations, and source pages before production use. The question text is original and paraphrased; each entry records a page supporting the fact or language answer.

The bank currently contains 20 questions: 14 English and 6 Swahili. The daily selector chooses seven English and three Swahili questions and stores their ordered IDs in D1. This is enough for local play and multiple possible daily sets, but the pool is small and will repeat. Expand and review the bank before a public launch.

## Review checklist

- Confirm every selected answer against the linked source.
- Check Swahili grammar, naturalness, and regional wording with a fluent reviewer.
- Check that each wrong choice is clearly wrong and no question has multiple defensible answers.
- Check the explanation against the question, including qualifications such as “hot desert” versus “largest desert.”
- Grow the bank enough to avoid frequent repeats while keeping the daily 7-to-3 language ratio.
- Record the reviewer and date before enabling production gameplay.
